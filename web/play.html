How to play

Fling

Drag anywhere and let go. Pull back like a slingshot; the dotted line shows exactly where you will fly. A longer pull means more power. You can change how much pull you need in Settings.

Hold on

You catch blooms automatically when you touch them, and you stick to glowing moss. Bare rock bounces you off, and you can rest on a ledge and fling again from there.

Dash

In mid-air, drag again to dash once. Time slows while you aim. It recharges every time you land or catch.

Read the danger

Thorns, spores, lasers, saws, lava and falling stalactites end your run. Everything that moves keeps a steady beat and warns you before it fires. Wait on a bloom, watch the rhythm, then go.

The deeper you go

The cave changes every few hundred metres, and every place has its own trick. Moss that grabs you, gears that keep time, water that slows your fall, heat that drags at you, fog you can barely see through, cold that freezes your grip, stones that pull you off course, ground that shakes on the beat. The first time you meet something new, a hint tells you what it does.

Weather

In Climb, fronts roll through: a fog bank, a gale, a cold snap, a blackout. The bar at the top tells you what's coming and for how long.

Cannons and slings

Climb into a barrel and it fires you along its arrow. A sling wheel spins you round; tap when the arm lines up with the marker to let go.

Score

Climbing, clean catches, rings, close calls and secrets build a combo up to x8. Bumping bare rock breaks it.

Seeds, ghosts and the Daily

Every run has a seed code. Play the same code and you get the same climb, with your best run racing you as a ghost. Send a code to a friend and you're playing the exact same cave.
