What's inside

Simple to pick up. Hard to put down.

One thumb, one move: drag to aim and let go to fling. Everything else is about where you fling to, and when.

23

Worlds with their own rules

Deep water that slows every fling, heat that saps your strength, cold that freezes you if you stay still, lodestones that bend your flight, and ground that quakes on a beat.

Cannons, sling wheels and zip lines

Hop in a barrel and fire when it lines up. Ride a spinning arm and let go on the marker. Catch a handle and fly off the end of the cable.

Pick a boon as you climb

Every few hundred metres the climb stops and offers three boons: a second air dash, a shield that comes back, a feather that lets you rise again.

Loot, jackpots and a daily wheel

Smash crates on the way up, light three targets for a jackpot, and take one free spin of the prize wheel every day.

69 looks to unlock

Patterned skins, hats, trails, catch bursts and companions, from a sprout leaf to a dragon. Win them from chests or buy them with shards. None of it changes how the game plays.

A new climb every day

Everyone gets the same Daily climb with a twist on top, like fog or low gravity. Keep a streak going and race your own ghost on any seed.

Stage select

Plus the Clockwork Spire, the Sunforge, the Lodestone, the Faultline, the Starwell and eight more.

How to play

Three moves to learn

Drag back

Touch anywhere and pull, like a slingshot. The dotted line shows exactly where you will land. A longer pull flings harder.

Let go

You stick to every bloom you touch. Rest there, watch the hazards keep their beat, and go when the way is clear.

Dash in the air

Drag again mid-flight to dash once. Time slows while you aim. It comes back every time you land.

Get Afterglow on every screen you own

Free, with no account and no app store needed. Once it is installed it plays with no connection at all. Your device is highlighted below.

Android

APK for phones and tablets
Download APK
  1. Tap Download APK and open the file when it finishes.
  2. If Android asks, allow Install unknown apps for your browser, then go back.
  3. Tap Install. If Play Protect says it does not recognise the app, tap More details, then Install anyway.

Android 7.0 or later. To update, download the new APK and install it over the old one.

Your device

Windows

Installer for PCs and laptops
Download for Windows Portable, no install
  1. Run Afterglow-Setup.exe. It installs in a few seconds and adds a Start menu and desktop shortcut.
  2. If Windows shows Windows protected your PC, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Play with the mouse, or aim with arrows and fling with Space. F11 goes full screen.

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.

Your device

iPhone and iPad

Home Screen app
Open the game
  1. In Safari, tap Open the game.
  2. Tap Share square with an arrow. It is at the bottom on iPhone and at the top right on iPad.
  3. Tap Add to Home Screen, then Add. Afterglow gets its own icon, opens full screen and plays offline.

Why is there no download? Apple only lets iPhone and iPad install apps from the App Store, so APK and EXE files cannot run there. The Home Screen app is the same game, and it works without a connection.

iOS and iPadOS 16.4 or later.

Questions

Before you climb

Is it free?

Yes. Every world, mode and mechanic is free. Shards for the shop come from playing. The only ad is optional: you can watch one to revive and carry on from your last hold.

Do I need an account?

No. There is nothing to sign up for. Your records, shards and cosmetics are saved on your device.

Why can't I install the APK on my iPhone or iPad?

APK files only run on Android. Apple only allows App Store apps on iPhone and iPad, so there Afterglow installs from Safari as a Home Screen app. It is the same game, full screen and offline.

Android or Windows warned me about the download. Is it safe?

Yes. The warning appears for any app that does not come from the Play Store or carry a paid publisher certificate. Each file is built automatically from the public source code on GitHub.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The Android and Windows apps carry the whole game inside them. The Home Screen and desktop web apps save themselves the first time you open them.

Which devices does it run on?

Android 7 and later, Windows 10 and 11, iPhone and iPad on iOS 16.4 or later, Macs, Linux and Chromebooks. Any recent browser can also play it without installing.

How do I move my progress to another device?

Progress stays on the device you play on, so each device keeps its own save. On iPhone and iPad, install the Home Screen app first, then play there, because Safari and the Home Screen app keep separate saves.
